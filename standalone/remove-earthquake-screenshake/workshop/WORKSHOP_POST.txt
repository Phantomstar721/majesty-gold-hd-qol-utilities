[h1]Remove Earthquake Screenshake[/h1]

Earthquake no longer shakes the main map or knocks your Tracking Windows off their targets.

This tiny quality-of-life mod removes only Earthquake's stock camera-shake call. Damage, particles, sound, duration, and targeting remain unchanged.

[list]
[*]Stops the main-view earthquake shake
[*]Keeps Tracking Window targets locked in place
[*]Works through the normal Majesty Gold HD launcher and Mods selector
[*]Includes registered GPL source for script-merging tools
[/list]

[b]Compatibility:[/b] Another mod that replaces [i]Earthquake_Unit_Created[/i] must be merged with this one rather than stacked by load order.
